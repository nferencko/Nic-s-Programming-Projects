Reflection

What I was able to get the AI to create an artifact quickly and all I had to do was understand the assignment and know what tools I wanted to use. It was a positive experience for me because all I had to focus on was creating a proof of concept that I wanted to create, and from there Claude took care of all the syntactical sugar which was needed due to me having no experience in html. The speed of getting something that fulfilled the basic requirements for the assignment made me motivated to go the extra mile and give it more of a personal touch and make it look better. I wanted the AI to give me different coloring themes for the website, and I would pick my favorite. However, it interpreted in a way that the website has different buttons on it and the viewer can click each button to change the theme. Despite the miscommunication, I actually really liked that after the fact and decided to keep it. here.
